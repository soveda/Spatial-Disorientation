Spatial Disorientation

Twin Orbits · 0.1.0-alpha3-hrtf · two sources moving around your head

Connect your Workshop Computer

Use a USB-C data cable. Close Serial Monitor/other apps. Use Chrome or Edge on desktop with MIDI and SysEx permission; iOS is unsupported. Select “Spatial Disorientation” for both MIDI ports below.

Connect the card, then enable MIDI.

A: turquoise · B: amber. Headphones recommended.

Main: position · X: speed/direction (centre stops) · Y: distance
Switch up: linked · middle: opposing · down: reset phase

Twin Orbits settings

Apply changes the running sound. Save to card stores the applied settings and briefly fades the audio out. Physical knobs remain independent performance controls.

Only Twin Orbits is implemented in this prototype. Elevation and the other startup functions are planned. Horizontal ear filters derive from Bill Gardner and Keith Martin’s MIT KEMAR measurements (MIT Media Laboratory, 1994), with separate timing and room cues. This is a short-filter approximation, not personalized HRTF rendering.
