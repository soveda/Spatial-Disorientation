Spatial Disorientation 0.4.0-beta3

A two-source headphone spatializer for Workshop Computer. Twin Orbits, Spatial Mixer and Disorientation with continuous height, vertical movement and sloping Fig8, Pendulum and Wander paths. 192MHz, two sources, all64HRTFtaps. Orbits has height/slope and two 8mu fader pages; repeated-save sampling is fixed. Direct pitch/roll positioning follows the lowered edge; yaw is unused.

  • Download beta firmware
  • Open matching beta editor — Chrome/Edge, USB-C data cable and MIDI/SysEx permission.
  • Controls and migration guide
  • Tester protocol and feedback template
  • Firmware checksum / firmware licenses and attribution

Normal boot is Twin Orbits. Hold Down on reset, select with Main (low: Orbits/noLEDs; middle: Mixer/leftLEDs; high: Disorientation/rightLEDs), then release.

Export old presets before beta Save to card. Beta1 cannot read beta3 flash8 or presetJSON7; Apply-only testing leaves old saved flash intact. For slope alone: centre0°, vertical excursion0°, path tilt40°, then Apply.

The user reports pitch/roll alpha2 tests pass; beta3 preserves those firmware bytes. Orbits D tap selects normal/height pages (8mu LEDs1–3/6–8 briefly identify them); D hold retains diagnostics. Randomise/Reset elevation at the bottom of the editor stage changes until Apply. Historical timing margins are narrow; collect new peaks on your setup. Report timing, listening, preset/USB/8mu results on your setup. Generic HRTFs vary by listener/headphones.
